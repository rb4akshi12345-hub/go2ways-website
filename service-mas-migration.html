Overview

Maximo 7.6 is behind you.
MAS is next.

IBM base support for Maximo 7.6.1 ended in September 2025. MAS brings Maximo Manage together with Health, Monitor, Predict and Mobile on Red Hat OpenShift, licensed with AppPoints. We plan the move so your operations keep running throughout.

  • Readiness assessment of customisations and integrations
  • AppPoints licensing planned up front
  • Full rehearsal in a non-production environment
  • Cutover plan with rollback and hypercare

What we deliver

Everything you need,
from one team

Readiness assessment

An inventory of customisations, integrations, reports and licences, with effort and risk for each.

AppPoints planning

OpenShift platform setup
